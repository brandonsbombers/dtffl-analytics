Tiers Breakdown

League-specific S-F tiers by season-to-date Toledo PPG -- not equal-sized buckets.

How Tiers Work

S = true positional elites  ·  A = high-end, just below elite  ·  B = strong weekly starter  ·  C = ordinary/solid starter  ·  D = marginal starter / useful depth  ·  E = bench-level but still rosterable  ·  F = waiver/replacement-level.

  • Tiers are built from your Toledo league scoring (season-to-date PPG), not generic fantasy points.
  • Tiers are not equal-sized buckets -- a tier's size reflects real gaps in production, not a fixed count.
  • E is the last rosterable tier; F is waiver/replacement level.
  • Rosterability targets: QB 20 · RB 42 · WR/TE 65 · K 14 -- players statistically similar to the nominal cutoff can stay rosterable past the target.
  • FLEX compares RB, WR, and TE directly against each other, not each player's own positional tier.
  • Out / IR / PUP / Suspended players are forced to F regardless of season production -- they can't help you this week. Questionable (Q) and Doubtful (D) players keep their production-based tier but show a warning badge.
  • Green border = Brandon's Bombers, blue border = free agent -- both based on this week's actual ownership, not an older snapshot.
